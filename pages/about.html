About this catalog

A single place to find proprietary language data that is suitable for training and evaluating language models.

The catalog is a central registry of datasets for researchers, engineers and organizations working on natural language processing, speech recognition and large language models for languages with few digital resources. Data is licensed from broadcasters, publishers, government institutions and community organizations, for use by approved researchers and commercial subscribers.

Purpose

Provide one point of discovery for available language data, so people do not have to hunt across scattered sources.

Data types

Audio such as radio broadcasts, podcasts and recorded surveys. Text such as news articles, books and educational materials. Transcriptions paired with audio for speech recognition.

Access

All metadata can be browsed freely. Getting the data requires an application, approval, and acceptance of the terms. See how to get access.

Contributing

New data is welcome. If your organization produces content in these languages and would like to make it available, please get in touch to discuss ingestion and licensing.

Citation

If you use data from the catalog in your work, please cite the catalog and the individual data providers.

Ethics and privacy

Recordings that could identify people are reviewed for personal information and anonymized. All data is shared under terms agreed with the originating institutions, and users must follow them.
