    Suggested outline for an access agreement

    Data access agreements usually cover these topics. Use them as a checklist when drafting.

    1. Parties, acceptance and authority
    2. Eligibility, approved proposal and scope of use
    3. Definitions (user, dataset, data, model, derived outputs)
    4. License grant, scope and restrictions
    5. Data handling, security and retention
    6. Model training, derived outputs and release
    7. Publication and attribution
    8. Monitoring, audit and enforcement
    9. Termination
    10. Intellectual property
    11. Indemnification, disclaimer and limits of liability
    12. Dispute resolution and governing law
    13. Entire agreement and severability
