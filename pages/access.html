How to get access

Anyone can browse the catalog. Getting the data itself requires approval, and the terms depend on who you are.

  1. Apply. Choose the application for your group.
  2. Wait for approval. A person reviews each application.
  3. Get a token. Once approved, sign in to get an access token for the API.
  4. Use the data. Request files and bundles through the API.

Researchers

Researchers, academic institutions, and organizations working on AI for social good can apply for access.

  • Submit a research proposal.
  • Demonstrate ethical data handling practices.
  • Commit to open-source publication of derived insights and models.

Applications that show potential for significant impact on development challenges are prioritized. They are reviewed on a rolling basis, and the typical response time is 2–3 weeks.

Commercial subscribers

Organizations that want to use the data in commercial products or services.

  • Agree to commercial terms.
  • Use a separate application form.
  • Larger daily download limits.

The commercial terms are set out in the legal documents.

What you receive

Data is delivered with protections that keep the original publications safe while staying useful for building models.

  • Text arrives as shuffled paragraphs rather than whole documents.
  • Audio and video arrive paired with their transcriptions, singly or as bundles.
  • Daily limits apply to text downloads. Commercial subscribers have larger limits.
